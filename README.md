# Alika Parks

I build open tools and methods for hard problems in scientific computing.

- **[conserved-field-compression](https://github.com/alikamp/conserved-field-compression)**: a closed-form layer that makes mass and energy exact after lossy compression. On NYX cosmology data at 1,304× compression, energy drift drops from 3.1×10⁻³ to 2.3×10⁻¹², nine orders of magnitude. Paper submitted to IWBDR-6 at IEEE BigData 2026.
- **[Parks Node Ejection Protocol](https://github.com/alikamp/Parks-Node-Ejection-Protocol)**: one geometric scalar, H = σ²/(1+σ²), predicts ejection timing and identity in chaotic 3- and 4-body systems, cutting compute by ~99% versus brute force.
- **[p53 Mechanical Selectivity](https://github.com/alikamp/p53-mechanical-selectivity)**: 3–5 pN of sustained force yields ~100× kinetic discrimination between mutant and wild-type p53 (provisional patent 64/121,139).
- **[KPBM](https://github.com/alikamp/kpbm-workspace)**: a field-local stabilizer that keeps lattice-Boltzmann fluid solvers stable at Re ≥ 1500 - Pair with compressor = cluster class runs on consumer hardware.
- **[PTSF Engine](https://ptsf-engine.vercel.app)**: a live SAT engine that resolves SAT Competition 2024 instances Kissat 4.0.4 cannot.
